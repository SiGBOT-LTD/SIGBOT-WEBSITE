Step 02

Point Sigbot at one inbox.

Sigbot reads signature blocks to pull out contact details. It never sends, replies, moves, or deletes anything, and you can disconnect an inbox whenever you like.

  1. 01

    Outlook — one click

    Sign in with Microsoft and approve access. Nothing to configure, and no password ever reaches us.

  2. 02

    Gmail & any IMAP inbox

    Connect with your server address and an app password. This is how Gmail, Fastmail, and self-hosted inboxes connect.

    Not your account password — your provider generates a separate one for apps.

    Sent straight to Sigbot over TLS. The password is never kept in your browser.

  3. 03

    Forward instead

    Don't want to connect an inbox at all? You get a private forwarding address. Anything you send it gets read and filed.

    you-4f2a@in.sigbot.co
