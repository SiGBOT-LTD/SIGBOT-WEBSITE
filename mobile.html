Common questions

When can I get the app?

The Android app arrives on Google Play first, with the iOS App Store to follow — both are coming soon. Your account will work in the app the day it lands; in the meantime the web app has everything, from any browser.

Is the mobile app included in my plan?

Yes — the mobile app and cloud sync are included on every plan, Free included. Business-card scanning is part of the Individual plan and up. See pricing for the full comparison.

Do I need a separate account?

No. It's one account and one database everywhere — log in on mobile with the same details you use at sigbot.app and your contacts, folders, and plan come with you.
