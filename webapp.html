Under the hood

Every signature is read twice.

A structural parser works through each signature in fourteen passes — and the messy ones go to an AI reader as well. The two results are merged field by field, weighted by confidence, so a mangled mobile signature still comes out as a clean, complete contact.

Common questions

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and processed under our GDPR/PIPEDA-compliant DPA. See our Security Overview.

Which inboxes does Sigbot support?

Outlook connects in one click with your Microsoft account. If you'd rather not connect an inbox at all, forward mail to your private Sigbot address instead — that works with any mailbox. Gmail and IMAP support are coming soon; we'll announce it in the app when they're live.

How fast do contacts appear after I connect?

Within minutes. Sigbot backfills your existing inbox and then extracts new contacts in real time as emails arrive.

Can I bring in contacts I already have?

Yes. Import CSV, Excel, or vCard files through a guided wizard with column mapping, or drag .eml and Outlook .msg files onto the app to have their signatures parsed like live mail. Exports work the same way in reverse — CSV or Excel, whole database or a filtered view.

How does team sharing work?

The Starter plan gives up to 5 people one shared database with team folders; Professional scales to 50 seats and adds per-folder permissions, so you decide who may view, edit, or manage each folder. See pricing for the full comparison.
