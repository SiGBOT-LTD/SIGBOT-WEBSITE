How a build works

Your part is a few minutes — plus, for a team build, a 20-minute call and the handover.

01

Connect the mailbox

You sign in to Sigbot and connect Outlook or Microsoft 365 — read-only, revocable any time. For a team build, each teammate gets a team invite and connects their own mailbox. Nothing is forwarded to us and no one at Sigbot reads your mail.

02

We run, clean and check the scan

The Inbox History Build's scan starts the moment you pay. The mailbox's history is scanned, signatures are parsed, contacts are merged by email address, and newsletters, no-reply senders and bounces are removed. Then a person at Sigbot checks the list before it's handed over. For team builds we also set up folders by project and trade and merge duplicates across people.

03

Handover

Your contacts are live in your Sigbot account, and your order page has the export: laid out for Procore's directory import or as a BuildingConnected bidder list, or plain Excel or CSV. The Inbox History Build includes 30 days of the Individual plan from delivery. Team builds include a 45-minute handover call.

Common questions

Do I need a Sigbot subscription?

Not for the Inbox History Build. It's a one-off, and you keep your contacts and the export whatever you decide next. You need a free Sigbot account — it takes a minute, and it's where the build is delivered — and the build includes 30 days of the Individual plan from delivery. The Team Database Build is for teams already on Starter or Professional; the 60-day pilot includes Starter for your team while it runs.

Why do I connect the mailbox before paying?

Because that is the order that protects you. The scan runs inside your own account against a mailbox you connect — nobody at Sigbot ever reads your mail — so if your IT setup blocks it, we both find out before any money changes hands rather than after. Once it's connected you can preview the first contacts it finds before you pay. If your IT team has to approve Sigbot first, save your brief and we'll send you the request to pass on; you pay only once the mailbox connects.

Which mailboxes can you build from?

The Inbox History Build works with Outlook and Microsoft 365 mailboxes. Gmail and other providers aren't supported yet: start the build, choose "Tell me when Gmail is ready", and we'll keep your answers and email you if that changes. For a team build, each teammate needs an Outlook or Microsoft 365 mailbox.

Who sees my email?

Nobody at Sigbot reads your mail. The scan runs inside your own Sigbot account against the mailbox you connected, and only the signature blocks are parsed. To check the result, a person at Sigbot looks at the contacts the build found — never your emails — and you agree to that when you order. You can revoke access the moment the build is delivered. See our security page and DPA.

What if the list isn't what I expected?

Tell us. What happens next depends on the build, and each one's terms are in our refund policy — separate from the money-back guarantee on paid plans.

Can I pay by invoice?

Yes. Create your free Sigbot account with the email address the invoice should go to, then book a call or email us — we'll send a Paddle invoice with bank transfer details, and your order updates by itself when it's paid.
