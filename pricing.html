Common questions

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan is free forever with no card required. Paid plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee: if you're not satisfied, email us within 30 days for a full refund.

What happens when I hit the Free plan's limits?

Free scans up to 150 emails a day and holds up to 1,000 contacts in total. Once you reach the contact cap, Sigbot stops scanning new emails. Your existing contacts are never deleted. Upgrade to Individual for unlimited contacts and 2,000 scans/day, plus a full backfill of your entire inbox history.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade whenever you like. Nothing is ever deleted on a downgrade: teams and folders beyond the new plan's allowance are archived read-only, and upgrading again restores them exactly as they were.

How often am I billed?

Individual is $190/yr or $19/mo — pick yearly or monthly at checkout. Starter and Professional are annual subscriptions, billed once a year. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and your first purchase is covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee.

What currency will I be charged in?

The prices above are already in your local currency — we detect your country and ask our payment provider, Paddle, what it will actually charge there, shown rounded to the nearest whole amount; the exact figure appears at the checkout. If we've guessed your country wrong, change it with the picker above the plans. Paddle is the merchant of record and handles local sales tax and VAT, including reverse charge for VAT-registered businesses in the EU and UK.

How does team pricing work?

Starter is one flat $2,500/year for up to 5 people sharing team folders and a single contact database. Need a few more? Add seats to Starter at $450/seat/year, up to 15 in total — an 8-seat team pays $3,850/year. Professional is $10,000/year for up to 50 people, multiple teams, and per-folder permissions. Beyond 50 seats, talk to us about an Enterprise plan — from $25,000 a year.
