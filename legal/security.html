1. Infrastructure

Sigbot is built on established cloud infrastructure rather than self-managed servers, so the Services inherit the physical and network security controls of our providers:

Hosting

Google Cloud Platform, EU: data in Firestore eur3, processing in europe-west2 (London)

Application backend

Firebase (Google)

Payments

Paddle (merchant of record)

2. Encryption

  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS.
  • Data at rest is encrypted using our cloud providers' standard at-rest encryption.
  • API keys and credentials are stored using secure secrets management.
  • Mailbox OAuth tokens are additionally encrypted with an application-level AES-256-GCM key held in Secret Manager.

3. Authentication and Access

Your account

  • You sign in with an email address and password, or with Google or Microsoft where enabled. Signing in with a provider grants identity only; it does not give Sigbot access to your mailbox.
  • Two-step verification with an authenticator app is rolling out to every account (Account details → Security). A team owner can require it for admin actions — see Roles and permissions.
  • Passwords must be at least 8 characters, with a lower-case letter, an upper-case letter and a number. The policy is enforced server-side.
  • Your email address must be verified before Sigbot will scan a mailbox.
  • You can sign out every other device from Account details → Security. Changing your password also signs out other sessions.
  • Deleting your account and signing out other devices both require a recent sign-in.
  • Every scan and OAuth action is rate-limited per account.
  • Requests from the web app carry an App Check attestation (reCAPTCHA Enterprise). App Check currently runs in monitor mode.

Mailboxes and internal access

  • Where supported by your email provider, Sigbot connects to inboxes via OAuth rather than storing your email password directly.
  • Internal access to production systems and Customer Data is limited to personnel who need it to operate or support the Services, on a role-based basis.
  • Multi-factor authentication is required for all staff accounts with production access.

4. Monitoring and Logging

We log access to production systems and monitor for anomalous activity. Application logs are kept for 30 days and admin-activity logs for 400 days (see section 9); these periods are reviewed in line with our data minimisation obligations.

5. Vulnerability Management

We welcome reports of potential security issues through our Vulnerability Disclosure Policy. Independent security testing is planned for launch; the cadence will be confirmed and published here once finalised.

6. Incident Response

We maintain an internal process for identifying, containing, and notifying customers of security incidents affecting their data, consistent with the notification commitments in our Data Processing Addendum (including 72-hour notification for confirmed personal data breaches under UK/EU GDPR).

7. Subprocessors

A current list of subprocessors that may process Customer Data is maintained in our Data Processing Addendum.

10. Your data, self-serve

You do not need to contact us for any of the following.

  • Download my data — Account details → Your data. One JSON archive of your account, settings, connected mailboxes, team memberships, scan history and audit entries, plus a CSV of your contacts with their change history. The archive is assembled in your browser; nothing is stored on our servers or emailed.
  • Delete account — Account details → Danger zone. Requires a recent sign-in. Cancels your subscription, then erases your data as described in section 9. See Delete your account.
  • Delete permanently and Empty trash — in Trash. Erases the selected records, or everything in Trash, now rather than after 30 days.
  • Scan-rule purge — when you add an exclusion rule for an address or domain, tick Also remove existing matches to erase every record that matches it, together with its change history and review-queue entries. The rule then blocks the address from being filed again. Personal accounts: Import Emails → Scan rules. Teams: the Admin console, for roles with Manage scan rules (see Roles and permissions).

Requests about anything else, or about data held in someone else's account, go to legal@sigbot.co.

11. Contact

Security questions or concerns: security@sigbot.co. To report a vulnerability, see our Vulnerability Disclosure Policy.
